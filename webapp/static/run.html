About this view

Display is the real background-tile PPU output (4 shades, no sprites/window yet). "boot ROM" shows whether reads at 0x0000-0x00FF are still coming from the boot ROM overlay or have switched to the cartridge (happens when the boot sequence writes to the rBANK register at the very end).
